Who We Are

BeatKonductaz

BeatKonductaz is an independent organization dedicated to hip-hop and beat culture, connecting producers, DJs, artists and communities through music, events, showcases and original programming.

From producer battles and live performances to conversations about the people and history behind the music, BeatKonductaz documents and celebrates the culture.

Built on community, creativity and respect for the craft.
