Featured cultural event

The Rich Flip

August 15, 2026 12 PM – 8 PM Mill Hill Art Center Macon, GA

Macon's Own Little Richard Tribute was a full-day cultural arts experience presented by Kevin "SCENE" Lewis featuring BeatKonductaz.

Celebrating the legacy of Little Richard, the event brought together hip-hop, sound design, live art, conversation and community in Macon, Georgia.

The program paired a hip-hop culture discussion panel with a three-round sound design battle: One Man Army, The Remix and the Final Round.

The panel featured Maureen "Ma Dukes" Yancey, Toney Smith, Shawn J. Period, BocaFloja, Stanley Stewart and Dumi Right.

The sound design battle brought together producers from different backgrounds to celebrate creativity, originality and the culture of beatmaking.
